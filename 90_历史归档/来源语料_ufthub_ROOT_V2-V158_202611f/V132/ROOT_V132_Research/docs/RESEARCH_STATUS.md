# ROOT V132 Research audit

## Proven
- The included implementation numerically reproduces analytic Newtonian inverse-square scaling and a low-degree J2 potential gradient within the reported tolerances.
- A spherical finite-size Yukawa source has a different altitude profile from a point-mass Yukawa approximation.

## Not proven
- Any experimentally required fifth force.
- Any geometric derivation of U(1) x SU(2) x SU(3), quantum field spectrum, or gravitational constant G.
- Any UV-complete, anomaly-free quantum unification.

## Next decisive empirical test
1. Obtain independent satellite tracking / gradiometry observations and their covariance and metadata.
2. Fit conventional gravity parameters, time-variable tides and non-gravitational accelerations jointly with Yukawa parameters.
3. Perform blind held-out altitude / orbit tests and quantify likelihood ratio and confidence intervals with full covariance.
4. Reject candidate parameters that conflict with independently measured gravity and orbital dynamics.

## Dimensional checks
- [GM] = L^3/T^2; [GM/r^2] = L/T^2.
- [J2] = 1, [alpha] = 1, [lambda] = L.
- [Omega^2 r] = L/T^2.
- In c=1 convention T and L may be expressed in the same unit, but hbar and G remain independent dimensional constants.
