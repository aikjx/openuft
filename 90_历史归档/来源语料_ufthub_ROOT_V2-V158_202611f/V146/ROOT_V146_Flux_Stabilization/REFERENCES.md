# 参考文献和背景（均为已有理论）

1. **Kaluza–Klein tower and bubble nucleation in six dimensional Einstein–Maxwell theory**, European Physical Journal C 84 (2024), 449. 公开网页：<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-024-12833-3>。支持球面磁通、曲率与六维真空能的径向稳定化背景。
2. **Flux compactifications: stability and implications for cosmology**, JCAP 2004(09)005. 公开 DOI：<https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2004/09/005>。关于紧化模稳定性条件。
3. **Chiral compactification on Minkowski × S² of N=2 Einstein-Maxwell supergravity in six dimensions**, Physics Letters B (1984). <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0370269384905896>。已知六维到四维磁通紧化方案，不能当作 ROOT 独有创新。
4. **Spinors and the Dirac operator**, educational mathematical exposition, monopole zero-mode theorem: <https://georgedouzas.github.io/posts/fundamentals-of-mathematics-for-physicists/part-i-13-spinors-dirac-operator.html>。针对磁通上扭曲 Dirac 零模数学内容。
5. **de Sitter vacua and supersymmetry breaking in six-dimensional flux compactifications**, Phys. Rev. D 94 025025 (2016), <https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.94.025025>。超引力背景、异常抵消的 Green–Schwarz 耦合。

所有数值输出仅基于本研究包声明的输入常数与数值算法，**不是来自上述论文的数据拟合**。
