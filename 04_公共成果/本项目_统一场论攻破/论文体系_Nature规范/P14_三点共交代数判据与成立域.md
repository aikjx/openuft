## Abstract

Three-point unification of the gauge couplings — the condition that $\alpha_1^{-1}$, $\alpha_2^{-1}$ and $\alpha_3^{-1}$ meet at a single scale — is the load-bearing joint of every Grand Unified scenario, and it is the single criterion our own programme (C7) had recorded as *marginally satisfied*. Here we replace the empirical "the three lines look convergent" test by a necessary-and-sufficient algebraic statement, and then price the residual exactly.

At one loop, two trajectories $\alpha_i^{-1}(t)=a_i-b_i t/2\pi$ intersect at $t^*_{ij}=2\pi(a_i-a_j)/(b_i-b_j)$. Three lines are concurrent **iff** the three ratios $(a_i-a_j)/(b_i-b_j)$ are equal — a condition on the beta coefficients alone, not a numerical threshold. Solving constructively, strict concurrency demands $b_3^*=-3.02080803406$; the Minimal Supersymmetric Standard Model (MSSM) supplies $b_3=-3$, and the deficit $\delta b_3=-0.0208$ is the entire algebraic origin of the C7 gap.

Quantitatively: the pure-Standard-Model chain misses by $\Delta\ln M=9.1885$ ($M$ ratio $9784$); the MSSM chain misses by $\Delta\ln M=0.9843$ ($M$ ratio $2.676$), i.e. a **relative tolerance of 3.240%**. The engineering criterion in use is 5%, so C7 passes at 5%, fails at 3%, and fails at 1%.

We further locate the *exact* crossing point analytically: $M_{\rm SUSY}^{*}=54.96$ GeV, which lies **below** $M_Z=91.19$ GeV, i.e. inside the non-physical region. In the physical domain $M_{\rm SUSY}>M_Z$ the spread has **no interior minimum** and rises monotonically with the soft scale ($0.9688$ at 10 GeV to $2.5468$ at $10^5$ GeV). Consequently 1 TeV is not the precision optimum but the compromise forced by the hierarchy problem and the LEP chargino bound.

Finally, the minimal-norm threshold correction needed to make the three lines meet exactly is $\delta=(+0.01756,-0.04214,+0.02458)$, $\|\delta\|=0.0518$ — only **0.21%** of $\alpha_{\rm GUT}^{-1}=24.269$. The gap therefore lives at the $10^{-1}$ level in $\alpha^{-1}$ space and is amplified to a factor 2.676 by the $1/\Delta b$ lever; both ends of that amplification must be stated, and we state them.

We conclude that C7 is not a binary claim but a **three-parameter domain**: C7 ∈ {one loop, tolerance ≥ 3.24%, $M_{\rm SUSY}\lesssim 1$ TeV, $\|\delta\|\approx 0.052$}. Nothing in this paper is a TUFT prediction; all arithmetic is standard $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ one-loop renormalization-group arithmetic, and the negative results are registered as negative.

## Introduction

The unification of the three gauge couplings is the oldest quantitative promise of Grand Unification. Georgi and Glashow's $\mathrm{SU}(5)$ construction [1] and the renormalization-group analysis of Georgi, Quinn and Weinberg [2] established the template: extrapolate the three measured couplings with one-loop beta functions, ask whether they meet, and read the meeting scale as the scale of new physics. Dimopoulos and Georgi [3] and, later, the precise analyses of Amaldi and collaborators [4] refined the statement with two-loop running, threshold corrections and $\mathrm{SU}(5)$-breaking spectra.

Two features of this template deserve separate scrutiny, and they are the subject of this paper.

**First, the criterion is usually stated as a visual or numerical one.** In practice one plots three lines and asks whether they "converge", then declares success if the spread stays below some tolerance — 5% in our own engineering pipeline. A tolerance-based criterion is not wrong, but it hides where the incompatibility lives: in the beta coefficients, in the initial conditions, or in the thresholds. The algebraic form of the condition, by contrast, is exact and one-dimensional, and it tells you which parameter must move.

**Second, the amplification between coupling space and scale space is enormous.** A shift of a few hundredths in $\alpha_i^{-1}$ translates into a factor of order unity in $M_{\rm GUT}$, because $\ln M\propto 2\pi/(b_i-b_j)$. Reporting "2.7 times off in $M$" without reporting the corresponding $0.05$ in $\alpha^{-1}$ misrepresents both the severity and the remedy. Conversely, reporting "0.05 in $\alpha^{-1}$" without the factor 2.7 understates how far the spectrum must be tuned.

Our programme's criterion C7 — three-point concurrency of the MSSM chain — had been recorded as satisfied under the 5% tolerance, with the pure-Standard-Model chain failing 7 of 8 related tests. That record was correct as bookkeeping and incomplete as physics. This paper performs the missing audit: it derives the exact criterion, measures the strict residual, locates the exact crossing point, and prices the minimal remedy.

We emphasise at the outset what this paper is **not**. It does not propose a new unification model; it does not claim that three-point concurrency is achieved; and it does not write any result as a prediction of the TUFT geometric framework. It is an audit of a criterion that our own pipeline uses, performed with the same standard tools that the particle-physics community uses, and it reports three failures that the earlier record did not contain: a tolerance that happens to straddle the measured value, an exact crossing point that lies in the non-physical region, and a 0.3% discrepancy in $\Delta\ln M$ between this engine and an earlier internal record.

## Results

### Step 1 — The criterion, stated exactly

The one-loop renormalization-group equation for a gauge coupling is

$$\frac{d\,\alpha_i^{-1}}{dt}=-\frac{b_i}{2\pi},\qquad t=\ln\frac{\mu}{\mu_0},\qquad b_i=\frac{2}{3}\sum_{f}T(R_f)+\frac{1}{3}\sum_{s}T(R_s)-\frac{11}{3}C_2(G).$$

Hence $\alpha_i^{-1}(t)=a_i-\dfrac{b_i}{2\pi}t$, with $a_i\equiv\alpha_i^{-1}(\mu_0)$. The crossing of trajectories $i$ and $j$ is at

$$t^*_{ij}=\frac{2\pi\,(a_i-a_j)}{b_i-b_j}. \tag{1}$$

Three trajectories are concurrent **iff** $t^*_{12}=t^*_{13}=t^*_{23}$, which is equivalent to

$$\frac{a_1-a_2}{b_1-b_2}\;=\;\frac{a_1-a_3}{b_1-b_3}\;=\;\frac{a_2-a_3}{b_2-b_3}\;=\;\frac{t^*}{2\pi}. \tag{2}$$

Equation (2) is a **necessary and sufficient** condition, and it involves only the beta coefficients and the boundary couplings — no tolerance, no fit, no visual judgement. Note that a rescaling $a_i\to a_i+c$ leaves (2) invariant: the condition constrains the *differences*, so an overall offset of the three couplings is absorbed into the common meeting value and is not a failure mode.

Eq. (2) defines a one-dimensional locus in the three-dimensional space of beta coefficients. For fixed $b_1,b_2$ and fixed initial conditions, it can be solved constructively for $b_3$:

$$b_3^*=b_1-\frac{(a_1-a_3)(b_1-b_2)}{a_1-a_2}. \tag{3}$$

### Step 2 — Constructive solution: the beta coefficient that would work

Inserting the boundary values used throughout this programme — $M_Z=91.1876$ GeV, $\alpha_1^{-1}=59.00749395$, $\alpha_2^{-1}=29.57704821$, $\alpha_3^{-1}=8.445945946$ (CODATA-2018-anchored, PDG-2022-normalised; see Data availability) — and the MSSM coefficients $b_1=33/5$, $b_2=1$:

$$b_3^*=-3.02080803406. \tag{4}$$

Substituting (4) makes the spread of the three $t^*_{ij}$ vanish to below the 60-digit resolution of the engine. The criterion is therefore **constructive**: it is not merely a test that can fail, it is a statement that says exactly which number must change and by how much.

The MSSM supplies $b_3=-3$, so

$$\delta b_3=b_3^*-b_3^{\rm MSSM}=-0.0208 \tag{5}$$

— a 0.69% deviation in the third beta coefficient. This single number is the algebraic origin of the entire C7 gap. It is not numerical noise, not a truncation artifact, and not a threshold effect: it is the mismatch between the $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ matter content that the MSSM actually has and the content that exact concurrency would require.

### Step 3 — The pure-Standard-Model chain

Running the three couplings with $b^{\rm SM}=(41/10,\,-19/6,\,-7)$ up to the scale where two of them meet gives three widely separated crossings:

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| $M_{12}$ | $1.027\times10^{13}$ GeV |
| $M_{13}$ | $2.453\times10^{14}$ GeV |
| $M_{23}$ | $1.005\times10^{17}$ GeV |
| $\Delta\ln M$ (spread of $t^*$) | **9.1885** |
| $M_{\max}/M_{\min}$ | **9784** |

This is the quantitative content of the often-quoted statement that "the couplings do not unify in the Standard Model alone". A factor 9784 in scale corresponds to $\Delta\ln M=9.19$; the criterion (2) fails by nine units, i.e. by nine orders of magnitude in the exponential sense. The pure-SM chain is not marginally wrong; it is categorically wrong, and no threshold correction of the size that a GUT spectrum can supply ($\|\delta\|\sim0.05$) could repair it.

> **Provenance note.** An earlier internal record quotes $\Delta\ln M=9.164$ with $M_{12}=1.0003\times10^{13}$ and $M_{23}=9.5486\times10^{16}$ GeV. The present engine, using the same physical inputs but a different truncation depth in $\alpha_i(M_Z)$, obtains $9.1885$. The 0.3% difference in the spread is amplified by the exponential map into 2.7%–5.2% differences in the individual crossings ($M_{12}$: $+2.7\%$; $M_{13}$: $+3.5\%$; $M_{23}$: $+5.2\%$). Both sets fail criterion (2) by the same order of magnitude; we register the discrepancy and its amplification rather than harmonise it silently.

### Step 4 — The MSSM chain: strict residual

Between $M_Z$ and $M_{\rm SUSY}$ the couplings run with $b^{\rm SM}$; above $M_{\rm SUSY}$ they run with $b^{\rm MSSM}=(33/5,\,1,\,-3)$. Taking $M_{\rm SUSY}=1$ TeV:

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| $M_{12}$ | $9.7990\times10^{15}$ GeV |
| $M_{13}$ | $1.4767\times10^{16}$ GeV |
| $M_{23}$ | $2.6222\times10^{16}$ GeV |
| $\Delta\ln M$ | **0.9843** |
| $M_{\max}/M_{\min}$ | **2.676** |
| relative tolerance $\Delta\ln M/\langle\ln M\rangle$ | **3.240%** |

The three crossings now lie within a factor 2.7 of one another — a genuine improvement of nearly ten orders of magnitude in scale, and the reason the MSSM chain is the working hypothesis of this programme. But criterion (2) is an equality, and 3.24% is not zero.

### Step 5 — The tolerance is doing the work

| tolerance | verdict |
|---|---|
| 5% (engineering criterion C7a in use) | **pass** |
| 3% | **fail** (3.240% > 3%) |
| 1% | **fail** |

The engineering criterion in force is "logarithmic relative difference < 5%". The measured value is 3.240%. The criterion passes by a margin of 1.76 percentage points, which is to say that the "achievement" of C7 rests on a tolerance chosen to be loose enough to accommodate the residual, not on the residual being small. We regard this as a defect of record-keeping, and we correct it here: **C7 is satisfied at 5% and only at 5%.**

We do not, however, swing to the opposite error. A 3.24% residual in the relative spread of the unification scale is *small* by the standards of GUT phenomenology — threshold corrections from a realistic spectrum routinely move $\alpha_i^{-1}$ by amounts of order $0.1$–$1$, and two-loop running shifts the crossings by comparable amounts. The correct statement is that the MSSM chain is *quantitatively close* to concurrency and *not exactly at* concurrency, and that the difference is dominated by a single beta coefficient (Step 2).

### Step 6 — Sensitivity: the analytic lever

Differentiating (1) with respect to $L\equiv\ln(M_{\rm SUSY}/M_Z)$ and using $\partial a_i/\partial L=-b_i^{\rm SM}/2\pi$:

$$\frac{\partial\ln M_{\rm GUT}}{\partial\ln M_{\rm SUSY}}=1-\frac{\Delta b^{\rm SM}_{ij}}{\Delta b^{\rm MSSM}_{ij}}. \tag{6}$$

For the three pairs:

| pair | $\Delta b^{\rm SM}$ | $\Delta b^{\rm MSSM}$ | analytic $\partial\ln M/\partial\ln M_{\rm SUSY}$ | central difference | $\max|\Delta|$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | $7.2667$ | $5.6$ | $-0.2976190476$ | $-0.2976190476$ | $2.17\times10^{-54}$ |
| 1–3 | $11.1$ | $9.6$ | $-0.1562500000$ | $-0.1562500000$ | |
| 2–3 | $3.8333$ | $4.0$ | $+0.0416666667$ | $+0.0416666667$ | |

The analytic and numerical derivatives agree to 54 digits, which certifies (6) as an identity rather than an approximation. Two consequences follow immediately.

**The unification scale is anti-correlated with the soft scale** along pairs 1–2 and 1–3 (negative slope) and mildly correlated along pair 2–3. Raising $M_{\rm SUSY}$ therefore does not simply rescale the answer; it differentially moves the three crossings.

**The lever is not small.** $\partial\ln M/\partial b_3=2\pi(a_2-a_3)/(b_2-b_3)^2=8.298$. A shift of $10^{-3}$ in $b_3$ moves $M_{23}$ by $e^{0.0083}$, i.e. 0.83%; the 0.0208 deficit of (5) moves it by a factor $\exp(8.298\times0.0208)\approx1.18$ per unit, compounding to the observed 2.676 across all three. **A beta coefficient accurate to $10^{-2}$ produces a unification scale accurate to a factor of order 3.** Any claim of "exact three-point unification" that does not control $b_3$ to $10^{-3}$ is a factor-2 claim at best.

### Step 7 — The exact crossing point lies in the non-physical region

Setting $t^*_{12}(L)=t^*_{23}(L)$ and solving:

$$L_0=\frac{2\pi\left[\dfrac{a_1-a_2}{\Delta b_{12}}-\dfrac{a_2-a_3}{\Delta b_{23}}\right]}{c_{12}-c_{23}}=-0.50628396,\qquad c_{ij}\equiv\frac{\Delta b^{\rm SM}_{ij}}{\Delta b^{\rm MSSM}_{ij}}, \tag{7}$$

which gives

$$M_{\rm SUSY}^{*}=M_Z e^{L_0}=54.9616\ {\rm GeV}. \tag{8}$$

At this point the spread of the three $t^*_{ij}$ is exactly zero. **But $54.96\ {\rm GeV}<M_Z=91.19\ {\rm GeV}$.** The exact concurrency point lies inside the region where the "MSSM segment" would have to be entered below the $Z$ mass — a region excluded both by the definition of electroweak symmetry breaking and by the LEP chargino bound ($m_{\chi^\pm}\gtrsim95.9$ GeV [5]).

The consequence for the domain of validity is sharp. In the physical domain $M_{\rm SUSY}>M_Z$ the function $\Delta\ln M(M_{\rm SUSY})$ has **no interior minimum**: it rises monotonically, from $0.9688$ at 10 GeV through $0.9843$ at 1 TeV to $2.5468$ at $10^5$ GeV. The minimum of the spread over the whole line sits at the non-physical crossing (8). Therefore:

- 1 TeV is **not** the precision-optimal soft scale; lower soft scales unify better.
- 1 TeV is nevertheless the scale that the hierarchy problem selects [6], and the LEP bound forbids going below $\sim$100 GeV.
- The residual 3.24% is thus not a failure of the MSSM but a **structural consequence of placing the soft scale where naturalness and experiment require it**.

This is, we believe, the single most useful result of this paper: the C7 gap is not an unexplained numerical accident. It is the price of the hierarchy, computable in closed form, and located exactly at 54.96 GeV where no physics can live.

### Step 8 — The threshold budget: minimal-norm correction

To make the three lines meet exactly, one may add a common constant to each $\alpha_i^{-1}$ at the matching scale — the one-loop footprint of a heavy spectrum. The minimal-norm solution of "three points onto one" is

$$\delta=(+0.017557,\;-0.042137,\;+0.024580),\qquad \|\delta\|=0.051845,\qquad \max|\delta|=0.042137, \tag{9}$$

with the fitted common value $\alpha_{\rm GUT}^{-1}=24.2688$ and $\ln(M_{\rm GUT}/\mu_0)=33.0879$. After applying (9) the spread is zero to below the 60-digit limit, and $\sum_i\delta_i=-7.5\times10^{-60}$, i.e. the constant mode has been fully absorbed into $\alpha_{\rm GUT}^{-1}$ (as it must be, by the invariance noted in Step 1).

The magnitude is the point:

$$\frac{\|\delta\|}{\alpha_{\rm GUT}^{-1}}=\frac{0.0518}{24.269}=0.21\%. \tag{10}$$

A 0.21% correction is exactly what a GUT-scale spectrum with $O(1)$–$O(10)$ ratios between heavy and GUT masses supplies [7]. So the gap is *repairable within the standard framework* — but only by specifying the spectrum, not by adjusting the tolerance. And because of the lever of Step 6, that 0.21% becomes a factor 2.676 in $M$: the same number, read in two spaces, is both "small" and "large".

We state both readings, and refuse to choose between them for rhetorical convenience.

## Discussion

### What C7 actually is

The operational lesson is that C7 should stop being reported as a pass/fail flag. Its correct form is the four-entry tuple of E14.9, because each entry is a knob that a future calculation can turn, and because the tuple makes visible the trade-off between them: relaxing the tolerance buys "success" without changing any physics, while lowering $M_{\rm SUSY}$ genuinely improves concurrency at the cost of naturalness.

### Relation to the TUFT programme

This paper is an audit performed *inside* the standard framework, on a criterion that our programme's candidate equation relies on. It is a **structural negative closure** of one joint, not a positive result for any geometric unification: we derive no TUFT prediction here, and the engine's red-line field records this explicitly (`standard SU(N) one-loop RGE arithmetic only; never written as a TUFT prediction`). Three negative findings are registered:

1. The 5% tolerance straddles the measured 3.240%, so the earlier "C7 achieved" record was tolerance-supported rather than residual-supported.
2. The exact concurrency point sits at 54.96 GeV, below $M_Z$; in the physical domain there is no interior optimum, so "tune $M_{\rm SUSY}$ to unify better" has no interior solution to tune to.
3. The present engine and an earlier internal record differ by 0.3% in $\Delta\ln M$; both fail (2), but the discrepancy is recorded rather than smoothed.

### What would close C7 honestly

Three routes, in increasing order of cost:

- **Two-loop running.** Two-loop coefficients shift $t^*$ by amounts comparable to the one-loop residual; whether they help or hurt must be computed, not assumed. The present engine is one-loop by construction and says nothing about this.
- **Spectrum-resolved thresholds.** Replace the single $\|\delta\|=0.052$ by an explicit sum over the heavy spectrum, $\delta_i=\sum_H b_i^{(H)}\ln(M_H/M_{\rm GUT})/2\pi$. This is the *only* route that converts (9) from a budget into a prediction.
- **A changed beta content.** Accept the constructive solution (4): a $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ sector whose effective $b_3$ is $-3.0208$ rather than $-3$. This requires matter beyond the MSSM at an intermediate scale, and it is falsifiable by the same lever of Step 6.

Route 3 is the honest version of "fix the equation": it does not adjust the criterion, it changes the beta content and lets the criterion be satisfied exactly. We flag it as the open direction of this joint rather than claim it.

### Limitations

- One loop only; no two-loop, no threshold sum, no running of the soft parameters themselves.
- $\alpha_i(M_Z)$ are taken at fixed truncation; the 0.3% bookkeeping discrepancy shows that digit-level convention matters at the $10^{-3}$ level of $\Delta\ln M$.
- $M_{\rm GUT}$ is defined as the mean of the three crossings; alternative definitions (e.g. the $\chi^2$-optimal point) shift it at the percent level.
- The LEP chargino bound is quoted as a single number; the actual exclusion is spectrum-dependent.
- No statement is made about proton decay, which is the standard independent test of any unification scale; the companion paper P1 carries that anchor separately.

## Methods

**Arithmetic.** All loop integrals are one-loop truncated; beta coefficients are frozen as exact rationals (`Fraction`) and never as floats. Couplings, logarithms and exponentials are evaluated with `mpmath` at 60 decimal digits (`mp.dps = 60`). Float conversion happens only at print time.

**Criterion.** Eq. (2) is evaluated as an equality of three rational-argument ratios; the constructive inverse (3) is checked by substituting the result and re-evaluating the spread, and is accepted only if the residual is below $10^{-40}$ (i.e. below the resolvable floor at 60 digits).

**Scan.** $M_{\rm SUSY}$ is scanned logarithmically over $[10,\,10^5]$ GeV in 91 points ($10^{k/20}$). Monotonicity is asserted, and the interior minimum is searched for explicitly; its absence in the physical domain is asserted as a finding, not as a failure.

**Sensitivity cross-check.** The analytic derivative (6) is compared with a central finite difference at $L=\ln(1\,{\rm TeV}/M_Z)$; agreement is required to better than $10^{-20}$, and 54 digits are observed.

**Threshold budget.** The minimal-norm shift is the least-squares solution of $\min\|\delta\|$ subject to $a_i+\delta_i=c+u_i t$ (three lines to one point), solved in closed form via a $2\times2$ normal-equation system; the constant mode is verified to cancel to $10^{-60}$.

**Guards.** 17 assertions, each with an id, a boolean, a measured value and a note; the engine exits 0 only when all pass, and the JSON records `guard_pass`, `guard_total`, `all_pass`, `exit_code` and a `red_line` string.
