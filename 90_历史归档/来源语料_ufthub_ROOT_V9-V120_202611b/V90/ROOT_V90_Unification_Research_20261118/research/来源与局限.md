## 外部参考（2026-11-18 检索）

1. Physical Review D, 19 Oct 2026: *Gravity-induced entanglement is not a theory-independent witness of nonclassicality of the gravitational field*. https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/r8ry-sp35 。用于提醒纠缠—媒介量子化推断依赖模型前提。
2. Physical Review D, accepted 2 Nov 2026: *Matter-mediated entanglement by classical gravity under realistic matter dynamics*. https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/bg9v-ln16 。讨论真实物质动力学下替代通道的约束。
3. Physical Review D, accepted 1 June 2026: *Operational quantum field theoretic model for gravitationally induced entanglement*. https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/rvky-5ppb 。提供操作化 QFT 研究方向。
4. Physical Review D, 6 March 2026: *Can Newtonian gravity produce quantum entanglement?* https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/fv38-kgkb 。比较不同半经典、随机模型的纠缠行为。
5. Nature (2025), *Classical theories of gravity produce entanglement*. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09595-7 。存在后续学术争论；不能把一篇工作的模型外推为所有经典模型。
6. Scholarpedia, Donoghue et al., *Quantum gravity as a low energy effective field theory*: https://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Quantum_gravity_as_a_low_energy_effective_field_theory 。解释 $41/(10\pi)$ 代表性势修正及其低能适用范围。
7. JHEP (2022), *Graviton corrections to the Newtonian potential using invariant observables*: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP01%282022%29180 。强调规范不变及关系观测量。
8. Physical Review D (2026), *When does entanglement through gravity imply gravitons?*: https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/lq47-1hfg 。检查延迟传播、局域性和经典通信的前提。
9. 2026-09 量子自由落体相位观测： https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42685188/ 。已经观察到与引力及等效原理相关的量子相位，但不等于引力场量子化。
