$$[\hat{\kappa}, \hat{\tau}]_\star = i\hbar \cdot \underbrace{\{\kappa, \tau\}}_{=1} = i\hbar$$


$$\Delta\kappa \cdot \Delta\tau \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
