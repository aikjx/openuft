# ROOT V89 主要权威资料与论文边界

- NIST CODATA physical constants: https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/ 。程序使用 G=6.67430e-11 m3 kg-1 s-2、2019 SI 精确 c 和 h，标准不确定度未传播到全部合成曲线。
- John F. Donoghue, *Quantum gravity as a low energy effective field theory*（Scholarpedia）https://var.scholarpedia.org/article/Quantum_gravity_as_a_low_energy_effective_field_theory 。代表性低能势 1PN 系数 3 和量子一圈系数 41/(10π) 依赖特定匹配惯例。
- *Gravity as a Quantum Field Theory*, Symmetry 2023 https://www.mdpi.com/2073-8994/15/2/449 。概述低能 EFT 适用域及不等于 UV 完备。
- 2025 PRL 134 061501 *Distinguishable Consequence of Classical Gravity on Quantum Matter* https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.061501 。经典—量子具体模型的可比预测。
- Nature 646, 813–817 (2025) *Classical theories of gravity produce entanglement* https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09595-7 。提出经典几何背景与量子物质传播导致纠缠的模型解释；有后续论文争论。
- Phys. Rev. D 114, 025019 (2026-07-24) *When does entanglement through gravity imply gravitons?* https://doi.org/10.1103/lq47-1hfg 。延迟传播与可检验量。
- Phys. Rev. D 114, 084050 (2026-10-19) *Gravity-induced entanglement is not a theory-independent witness of nonclassicality of the gravitational field* https://doi.org/10.1103/r8ry-sp35 。信息论局域性与时空局域性区别。
- Phys. Rev. D accepted 2026-11-02 *Matter-mediated entanglement by classical gravity under realistic matter dynamics* https://doi.org/10.1103/bg9v-ln16 。针对特定机制给出强抑制结果；不可以此宣称已排除所有经典替代模型。
- 2026-02-04 Nature Communications *Bell correlations between momentum-entangled pairs of 4He atoms* https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69070-3 。是大质量物质运动自由度量子相关实验，不是观测到引力介导纠缠。
