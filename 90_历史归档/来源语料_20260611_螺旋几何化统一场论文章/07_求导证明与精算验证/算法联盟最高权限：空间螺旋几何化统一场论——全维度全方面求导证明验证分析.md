## 2. Mathematical Derivation

### 2.1 Space Spiral Geometry

**Parameter Equation**:
$$\mathbf{R}(\theta) = (\rho\cos\theta, \rho\sin\theta, b\theta)$$

**First Derivative** (tangent vector):
$$\mathbf{R}'(\theta) = (-\rho\sin\theta, \rho\cos\theta, b)$$

**Magnitude of tangent**:
$$|\mathbf{R}'(\theta)| = \sqrt{\rho^2\sin^2\theta + \rho^2\cos^2\theta + b^2} = \sqrt{\rho^2 + b^2} = \ell$$

**Arc length**:
$$s = \int_0^\theta |\mathbf{R}'(\phi)| d\phi = \ell\theta \quad \Rightarrow \quad \theta = \frac{s}{\ell}$$

**Second Derivative**:
$$\mathbf{R}''(\theta) = (-\rho\cos\theta, -\rho\sin\theta, 0)$$

**Magnitude of second derivative**:
$$|\mathbf{R}''(\theta)| = \sqrt{\rho^2\cos^2\theta + \rho^2\sin^2\theta} = \rho$$

### 2.2 Curvature and Torsion

**Curvature** (rate of change of direction):
$$\kappa = \frac{|\mathbf{R}' \times \mathbf{R}''|}{|\mathbf{R}'|^3}$$

Compute cross product:
$$\mathbf{R}' \times \mathbf{R}'' = \begin{vmatrix}
\mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\
-\rho\sin\theta & \rho\cos\theta & b \\
-\rho\cos\theta & -\rho\sin\theta & 0
\end{vmatrix} = (\rho b\sin\theta, -\rho b\cos\theta, \rho^2)$$

$$|\mathbf{R}' \times \mathbf{R}''| = \sqrt{(\rho b\sin\theta)^2 + (-\rho b\cos\theta)^2 + (\rho^2)^2} = \sqrt{\rho^2b^2 + \rho^4} = \rho\sqrt{\rho^2 + b^2} = \rho\ell$$

Thus:
$$\kappa = \frac{\rho\ell}{\ell^3} = \frac{\rho}{\ell^2} = \frac{\rho}{\rho^2 + b^2}$$

**Torsion** (rate of change of orientation):
$$\tau = \frac{(\mathbf{R}' \times \mathbf{R}'') \cdot \mathbf{R}'''}{|\mathbf{R}' \times \mathbf{R}''|^2}$$

Third derivative:
$$\mathbf{R}'''(\theta) = (\rho\sin\theta, -\rho\cos\theta, 0)$$

Dot product:
$$(\mathbf{R}' \times \mathbf{R}'') \cdot \mathbf{R}''' = (\rho b\sin\theta)(\rho\sin\theta) + (-\rho b\cos\theta)(-\rho\cos\theta) + (\rho^2)(0) = \rho^2b(\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta) = \rho^2b$$

Thus:
$$\tau = \frac{\rho^2b}{(\rho\ell)^2} = \frac{b}{\ell^2} = \frac{b}{\rho^2 + b^2}$$

### 2.3 Fine-Structure Constant Geometricization

**Key Identity**:
$$\alpha = \frac{\tau}{\kappa} = \frac{b/(\rho^2+b^2)}{\rho/(\rho^2+b^2)} = \frac{b}{\rho} = \tan\theta_0$$

Where θ₀ is the spiral pitch angle.

### 2.4 Geometric Scale Unification

$$\kappa^2 + \tau^2 = \frac{\rho^2}{(\rho^2+b^2)^2} + \frac{b^2}{(\rho^2+b^2)^2} = \frac{\rho^2 + b^2}{(\rho^2+b^2)^2} = \frac{1}{\rho^2 + b^2} = \frac{1}{\ell^2}$$
