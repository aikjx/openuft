PART 2: DIRECTION (VECTOR NATURE)
========================================================================

[2.1] Electromagnetic Force Direction
  Geometric mode: RADIAL
  Direction: perpendicular to helix axis
  E-field: radial oscillation (inward/outward)
  B-field: tangential rotation (azimuthal)
  Force direction: F = q(E + v x B)
  E perpendicular to B, both perpendicular to propagation

  Charge interaction:
    Like charges: radial oscillation in phase -> repulsion
    Unlike charges: radial oscillation out of phase -> attraction
  Vector: 3D spatial vector (E_x, E_y, E_z)

[2.2] Strong Force Direction
  Geometric mode: TANGENTIAL
  Direction: in plane perpendicular to radial, along circle
  Color field: tangential phase rotation
  Force: color gradient in tangential space

  Color interaction:
    Different colors: tangential phase difference -> attraction
    Same colors: tangential phase same -> repulsion
    Colorless combination (RGB): tangential phases cancel -> no force

  Vector: internal SU(3) space, not spatial direction
  8 gluon directions in color space

[2.3] Weak Force Direction
  Geometric mode: CHIRAL
  Direction: perpendicular to all 3 spatial directions (4th dimension)
  Weak field: chiral rotation
  Force: coupling to handedness

  Chiral interaction:
    Left-handed particles: couple to W, Z bosons
    Right-handed particles: no weak coupling
    Maximal parity violation

  Vector: not a spatial vector
  Scalar in space, but chiral in internal space
  V-A structure (vector - axial vector)

[2.4] Gravitational Force Direction
  Geometric mode: AXIAL
  Direction: along helix axis
  Gravitational field: axial pressure gradient
  Force: F = -m*grad(phi), always attractive

  Mass interaction:
    All masses attract (no repulsion)
    Axial mode is monotonic (no oscillation)
    No negative mass -> no repulsive gravity

  Vector: 3D spatial vector, always radial toward mass
  Unlike EM: always attractive, no charge

[2.5] Direction Summary Table
  +----------+------------+-------------------+------------------+

  | Strong   | Tangential | Internal (color)  | SU(3) vector     |
  | Weak     | Chiral     | Internal (chiral) | Scalar + chiral  |
  | Gravity  | Axial      | 3D spatial        | 3D vector        |

PART 5: RANGE (INTERACTION DISTANCE)
========================================================================

[5.1] Range Formula
  For massive mediator: range ~ hbar/(mc)
  For massless mediator: range = infinity

[5.2] Electromagnetic Range
  Photon mass: m_gamma = 0 (or < 10^-18 eV experimentally)
  Range: INFINITE
  Potential: V(r) = e^2/(4*pi*eps0*r) ~ 1/r
  Falls off as 1/r, never zero

  Geometric reason:
    Radial mode: extends to infinity
    No mass term: oscillation never damped
    Transverse wave: propagates forever

[5.3] Strong Force Range
  Gluon mass: m_g = 0
  Naive range: infinite
  BUT: color confinement!
  Effective range: ~ 1 fm = 10^-15 m
  Potential: V(r) ~ -alpha_s/r + sigma*r (Coulomb + linear)
  Linear term: confinement string

  Geometric reason:
    Tangential mode: confined in helix geometry
    Cannot separate: energy grows linearly
    'Range' is confinement radius, not mediator mass

[5.4] Weak Force Range
  W mass: M_W = 80.4 GeV/c^2
  Z mass: M_Z = 91.2 GeV/c^2
  Range: hbar*c/M_W ~ 2 x 10^-18 m
  Potential: V(r) ~ exp(-M_W*r)/(r) (Yukawa)
  Exponential decay: very short range

  Geometric reason:
    Chiral mode: broken by Higgs mechanism
    Mass acquired: chiral oscillation damped
    Range = 1/mass = coherence length

[5.5] Gravitational Range
  Graviton mass: m_graviton = 0 (expected)
  Range: INFINITE
  Potential: V(r) = -G*m1*m2/r ~ 1/r
  Falls off as 1/r, like EM
  BUT: always attractive, no shielding

  Geometric reason:
    Axial mode: extends to cosmological horizon
    No mass term: no damping
    Cumulative: all masses in universe contribute

[5.6] Range Comparison Table
  +----------+----------+---------------+----------------+
  | Force    | Mediator | Mass          | Range          |
  +----------+----------+---------------+----------------+
  | EM       | Photon   | 0             | Infinite       |
  | Strong   | Gluon    | 0 (confined)  | ~ 1 fm         |
  | Weak     | W, Z     | ~ 100 GeV     | ~ 10^-18 m     |
  | Gravity  | Graviton | 0 (?)         | Infinite       |
  +----------+----------+---------------+----------------+

[5.7] Geometric Interpretation of Range
  Radial (EM): extends to infinity
    -> Long range, 1/r potential

  Tangential (Strong): confined in helix
    -> Short range (confinement), not from mass

  Chiral (Weak): broken symmetry
