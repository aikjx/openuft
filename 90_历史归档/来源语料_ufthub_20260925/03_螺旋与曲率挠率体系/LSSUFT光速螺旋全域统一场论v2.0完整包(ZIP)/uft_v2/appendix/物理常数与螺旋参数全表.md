## 四、螺旋线数学恒等式

### 4.1 Frenet-Serret方程

$$
\frac{d}{ds}\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{T}\\\mathbf{N}\\\mathbf{B}\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}0&\kappa&0\\-\kappa&0&\tau\\0&-\tau&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\mathbf{T}\\\mathbf{N}\\\mathbf{B}\end{pmatrix}
$$

### 4.2 圆柱螺旋线参数

$$
\mathbf{r}(s) = \left(a\cos\frac{s}{L},\; a\sin\frac{s}{L},\; \frac{bs}{L}\right),\quad L=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}
$$

$$
\kappa = \frac{a}{a^2+b^2},\quad \tau = \frac{b}{a^2+b^2}
$$

$$
a = \frac{\kappa}{\kappa^2+\tau^2},\quad b = \frac{\tau}{\kappa^2+\tau^2}
$$

### 4.3 光速螺旋核心关系

$$
\omega = c\sqrt{\kappa^2+\tau^2} = c\kappa_0
$$
$$
v_\parallel = c\frac{\tau}{\kappa_0},\quad v_\perp = c\frac{\kappa}{\kappa_0}
$$
$$
E = \hbar\omega = \hbar c\kappa_0,\quad p = \hbar\tau,\quad m_0 = \frac{\hbar\kappa}{c}
$$
$$
\gamma = \frac{\kappa_0}{\kappa},\quad E^2 = p^2c^2 + m_0^2c^4
$$

### 4.4 静止粒子（τ=0）

$$
\kappa_0 = \kappa = \frac{1}{\lambda_C} = \frac{mc}{\hbar}
$$
$$
\omega_C = c\kappa = \frac{mc^2}{\hbar},\quad f_C = \frac{mc^2}{h}
$$
$$
a = \lambda_C = \frac{\hbar}{mc},\quad b = 0
$$
