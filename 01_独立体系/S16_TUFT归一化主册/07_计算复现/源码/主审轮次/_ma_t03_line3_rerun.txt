    H(T) = 1.66 * sqrt(g_star) * T^2 / M_Pl_red

  -- analysis --
  At T >> 100 GeV (EW scale and above): K >> 1, collisions dominate.
     SM plasma remains in thermal equilibrium.  No Sakharov-3 opening.
  At T ~ T_EW ~ 160 GeV: K still >> 1 for weak-scale processes.
     The ONLY way out-of-equilibrium arises at EW scale is a STRONG

  leptogenesis / high-reheating window: at T ~ 1e14-1e16 GeV the
  thermal bath has NOT yet relaxed to equilibrium, and heavy-particle
  decays can proceed out-of-equilibrium.  Below T_K1_highT, the plasma
  falls into equilibrium and stays there until the MeV scale.

  LOW-T four-Fermi decoupling (neutrino freezeout, Gamma ~ G_F^2 T^5):
    T_nu_freeze = (1/(G_F^2 M_Pl_red))^(1/3)

  (This is the ~1 MeV neutrino decoupling scale.  It is far below EW

  KEY: the high-T break at 1.58e+14 GeV means the reheating window 
  T_rh > T_K1_highT is an OUT-OF-EQUILIBRIUM epoch by itself.  If T_rh
  sits above this scale, Sakharov-3 is automatically satisfied during
  the initial thermalization -- no V(n,T) needed.  But this requires
  T_rh ~> 1e14 GeV, which is the GUT-scale leptogenesis regime, and
  TUFT has no prediction for T_rh (P17 sets H_inf, not T_rh).

[3] E273 AUDIT: static wall + detailed balance = zero net B-production
================================================================================

  Detailed balance theorem (thermal equilibrium):
    For any reaction  X <-> Y  with baryon numbers B_X, B_Y:
      rate(X->Y) = rate(Y->X)   in thermal equilibrium.
    Hence d(n_B)/dt = sum [rate(X->Y) - rate(Y->X)] * (B_Y - B_X) = 0.

  Consequences for a STATIC wall:
    (a) wall is time-independent: no flux through it, no moving boundary.
    (b) plasma on both sides at same T, same chemical potential mu_B=0.
    (c) even if B-violating sphalerons exist AND CP violation exists,
        detailed balance forces forward/back rates equal.
    (d) net baryon asymmetry produced = 0.

  This is EXACTLY the E273 verdict: static wall obeys detailed balance.
  The Sakharov-3 condition (departure from equilibrium) is VIOLATED
  by the static-wall configuration itself, independent of C/CP or B.

  What would be needed to escape:
    (i)   a MOVING wall (v_w > 0) sweeping through supercooled plasma,
    (ii)  supercooling: T_inside < T_outside by a non-negligible factor,
    (iii) wall velocity such that particles cannot equilibrate across it,
    (iv)  a phase transition that is genuinely FIRST ORDER (not crossover).
  All four require knowing V(n,T) -- which is MISSING.

[4] WHAT V(n,T) WOULD DECIDE -- and what its absence means
================================================================================

  V(n,T) = finite-temperature effective free energy of topological charge n.
  It is the temperature-dependent generalization of V(n).  Its derivatives
  determine the entire non-equilibrium pipeline:

    Quantity              Requires V(n,T)?   Why
    -------------------  -----------------  ----------------------------
    Critical temp T_c     YES              solve V(n=+,T)=V(n=-,T)
    Phase order (1st/crossover) YES       check latent heat / spinodal
    Supercooling (T_n/T_c) YES            spinodal decomposition temp
    Latent heat L        YES              L = -d(V_+-V_-)/dT at T_c
    Wall velocity v_w   YES              Jouguet detonation condition
    Wall thickness L_w  YES              interface profile from V''
    Out-of-equil param  YES              K_wall ~ L_w / (gamma v_w) / mfp
    Sphaleron rate on   YES              depends on vev(T), from V(n,T)
     broken side

  Verdict: EVERY quantitative handle on Sakharov-3 runs through V(n,T).
  With V(n,T) missing, none of T_c, supercooling, v_w, or wall departure
  can be computed from TUFT-internal resources.

[5] DE SITTER EPOCH (P17 Starobinsky): dilution argument
================================================================================

  During inflation: spacetime is de Sitter, H = const ~ H_inf.
  Any pre-existing baryon asymmetry Y_B_initial is diluted by exponential
  expansion: Y_B(a) ~ a^{-3} (number density redshifts as a^{-3}),
  while the inflationary volume grows as exp(3 N_e-folds).

  Minimum N_e-folds (flatness+horizon) = 60
  Volume dilution factor exp(3 N_e)    = 1.4894e+78
  Residual Y_B after inflation          = Y_B_initial / 1.49e+78 ~ 0

  => Any pre-inflation baryon asymmetry is erased to ~zero.
  => Baryogenesis MUST occur AFTER reheating (T < T_rh).
  => The de Sitter epoch itself cannot be the baryogenesis epoch:
     de Sitter has Gibbons-Hawking T_GH ~ H_inf/2pi but no thermal bath
     in the standard sense; particles are not in equilibrium with the
     horizon, and there is no defined baryon chemical potential.
