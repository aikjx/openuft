# 科研来源（2026-11-17 核查）

1. **NIST/CODATA 2022 常数：** https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/index.html ，包括 G=6.67430(15)×10^-11、定义精确的 c。2026 CODATA调整预计于2027年发布，尚不能引用为已发布新值。https://codata.org/initiatives/data-science-and-stewardship/fundamental-physical-constants/
2. **2026 扭秤G测量：** *Redetermination of the gravitational constant with the BIPM torsion balance at NIST*, Metrologia 63 025012，https://doi.org/10.1088/1681-7575/ae570f 。这类实验是测量G，不是从c推导G。
3. **2024 电磁—引力异常耦合的否定性实验：** Tajmar et al., *In-depth experimental search for a coupling between gravity and electromagnetism with steady fields*, Scientific Reports, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-70286-w 。采用电磁屏蔽与高精度平衡装置，所检验构型未发现纳牛顿/纳牛顿米量级异常。
4. **电磁能动张量：** Crowell, *Stress-energy tensor of the electromagnetic field*, https://phys.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Relativity/Special_Relativity_%28Crowell%29/10%3A_Electromagnetism/10.06%3A__Stress-energy_tensor_of_the_electromagnetic_field 。
5. **轴子与电磁场作用：** *Axion cloud decay due to axion-photon conversion with background magnetic fields*, PASJ 74, https://academic.oup.com/pasj/article/74/1/64/6457144 。
6. **轴子电动力学的E·B及场结构：** *Axion Electrodynamics and the Casimir Effect*, https://www.mdpi.com/2624-8174/6/1/27 。
7. **伪标量拓扑项度规独立的文献例：** *Anomalous character of the axion–photon coupling in a magnetic field distorted by a pp-wave gravitational background*, https://www.academia.edu/27630281/Anomalous_character_of_the_axion_photon_coupling_in_a_magnetic_field_distorted_by_a_pp_wave_gravitational_background 。
