# TUFT-r163｜宇宙的本源与粒子属性元数据及相互转换条件审计

**判词**：`ORIGIN-POSTULATES-MUTUALLY-EXCLUSIVE-PARTICLE-PROPERTY-TABLE-ABSENT-AND-NO-CONVERSION-CRITERIA`
