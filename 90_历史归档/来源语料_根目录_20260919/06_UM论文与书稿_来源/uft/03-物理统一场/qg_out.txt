========================================================================
DIMENSION 2: RENORMALIZATION FAILURE
========================================================================

[2.1] Why QED is renormalizable
  QED Lagrangian: L = psi-bar(i*gamma*d - e*A)*psi - (1/4)F^2
  Coupling: e (dimensionless)
  Photon: massless, spin-1
  Loops: integrals diverge as log(Lambda)
  Counter-terms: absorb divergences, finite parameters remain
  Result: PREDICTIVE THEORY

[2.2] Why gravity is NOT renormalizable
  GR Lagrangian: L = (1/16*pi*G) * R
  Coupling: G (dimensionful, [L]^2)
  Graviton: massless, spin-2
  Loops: integrals diverge as Lambda^2, Lambda^4, ...
  Counter-terms: need INFINITE new parameters
  Result: NON-PREDICTIVE at high energy

[2.3] Dimensional analysis
  Newton's constant: G ~ [L]^2 (in natural units)
  Effective coupling: G * E^2 (grows with energy)
  At Planck energy: G * E_P^2 ~ 1 (strong coupling)
  Above Planck energy: theory breaks down

  Gravity becomes strong at Planck scale
  Perturbation theory fails
  No way to 'sum' all divergences

  STATUS: Non-renormalizable = infinite parameters = non-predictive

========================================================================
DIMENSION 3: TIME PROBLEM - NO EXTERNAL CLOCK
========================================================================

[3.1] Standard quantum mechanics
  Time t is a PARAMETER, not an operator
  States evolve: psi(t) = exp(-i*H*t/hbar) * psi(0)
  Time is external, absolute, background
  Hamiltonian H generates time evolution
  Measurement at time t gives probability |psi(t)|^2

[3.2] General relativity
  Time is PART OF SPACETIME
  Metric g_mu nu determines proper time: d*tau = sqrt(-g)*dt
  No external time parameter
  Time is dynamical, curved, relative
  Different observers see different times

[3.3] The conflict
  QM: need external time t to define evolution
  GR: time is part of the dynamical geometry
  Combine: WHICH time to use?

  Option A: Pick one coordinate time -> breaks diffeomorphism
  Option B: Use proper time -> observer-dependent, not global
  Option C: Wheeler-deWitt equation -> H*psi = 0, no time!

  Wheeler-deWitt: 'The problem of time'
  Wavefunction of universe psi[g]
  H_universe * psi[g] = 0
  No time evolution, just constraint
  How to recover dynamics? UNKNOWN

  STATUS: Time problem is conceptual, not just technical

========================================================================
DIMENSION 5: BLACK HOLE INFORMATION PARADOX
========================================================================

[5.1] Black hole thermodynamics
  Hawking radiation: black holes emit thermal radiation
  Temperature: T_H = hbar*c^3/(8*pi*G*M*k_B)
  Entropy: S_BH = A/(4*l_P^2) = k_B*c^3*A/(4*G*hbar)

  For solar mass black hole:
  M = 2e30 kg
  T_H = 6.137388e-08 K
  Very cold, but thermal!

[5.2] The paradox
  Pure state collapses -> black hole (pure state)
  Hawking radiation -> thermal (mixed state)
  Thermal radiation = maximal information loss
  Pure -> Mixed: VIOLATES UNITARITY

  Unitary evolution in QM:
  Pure state -> Pure state (always)
  Information is CONSERVED

  Black hole evaporation:
  Pure -> Mixed -> Nothing
  Information is LOST

  CONTRADICTION!

[5.3] Proposed resolutions
  1. Information escapes in Hawking radiation (unclear how)
  2. Remnant left behind (problems with infinite states)
  3. Firewall at horizon (breaks equivalence principle)
  4. Holography (AdS/CFT) - but our universe is not AdS
  5. Many-worlds: all outcomes exist (which one is 'ours'?)

  None universally accepted

  STATUS: Information paradox is concrete obstruction to QG

DIMENSION 6: COSMOLOGICAL CONSTANT PROBLEM
========================================================================

[6.1] Vacuum energy from QFT
  Vacuum has zero-point energy: E_0 = sum(i) (1/2)*hbar*omega_i
  Sum over all modes up to cutoff Lambda
  E_0 ~ Lambda^4 (quartic divergence)

  If Lambda = m_P (Planck scale):
  rho_vac ~ m_P^4 = (2.176434e-08)^4 = 2.243790e-31 GeV^4
  In SI: rho_vac ~ 10^112 J/m^3

[6.2] Observed dark energy
  Omega_Lambda = 0.69 (69% of universe)
  rho_DE = Omega_Lambda * rho_critical
  rho_critical = 3*H0^2/(8*pi*G)
  rho_c = 8.532855e-27 kg/m^3
  rho_DE ~ 10^-9 J/m^3

[6.3] The discrepancy
  Theoretical: 10^112 J/m^3
  Observed:    10^-9 J/m^3
  Ratio:       10^121
  This is the 'worst prediction in physics'

[6.4] Why this blocks quantum gravity
  Lambda = 8*pi*G*rho_vac (GR equation)
  If QFT vacuum energy is real:
  Lambda ~ 10^112 J/m^3
  Universe would have curved to Planck size instantly
  We would not exist

  But we exist, Lambda is tiny
  How? Cancellation to 10^-121 precision?
  Or: vacuum energy doesn't gravitate? (why?)
  Or: quantum gravity modifies GR? (how?)

  Without solving this, quantum gravity predictions are wrong by 120 orders

  STATUS: Cosmological constant is 10^121 problem for QG
