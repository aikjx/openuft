## 八、判词与遗留

**判词**：ANCHOR-POWER-LAW-ALPHA-SQUARED / TWO-ALPHA-DOUBLE-COUNTING / ONLY-COMPTON-ANCHOR-CLOSES / MASSFORM-DIMENSION-BLIND-TO-ALPHA —— 玻尔锚质量 = α²√(1+α²) m_e 是闭式；α 被数两次；唯一闭合锚 λ̄_C；量纲门对 α 全盲。
