Q5 FIX: Correct Electron Charge Formula
========================================================================

[THE BUG]
  WRONG:  e = sqrt(2*alpha*eps0*hbar*c)
  RIGHT:  e = sqrt(4*pi*alpha*eps0*hbar*c)
  (4*pi is correct for Coulomb constant)

[CORRECT VERIFICATION]
  e_wrong = sqrt(2*a*eps0*hbar*c) = 6.391759999530e-20
  e_right = sqrt(4*pi*a*eps0*hbar*c) = 1.602176633948e-19
  e_exp  = 1.602176634000e-19
  e_wrong err = 60.11%
  e_right err = 0.000000%

[WHY 4*pi?]
  Coulomb constant: k = 1/(4*pi*eps0)
  Fine structure: alpha = e^2/(4*pi*hbar*c)
  Therefore: e^2 = 4*pi*alpha*hbar*c
  So: e = sqrt(4*pi*alpha*hbar*c)
