\section{Units and motivation}
We use $c=1$ only, keeping $\hbar$ and $G$ dimensionful and independent. The attempted geometric unification is an explicit comparison to classical Kaluza--Klein theory, not a derivation from purely three-dimensional helical motion.
\section{Five-dimensional null helix}
With $ds_5^2=-dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2+R^2d\phi^2$ and $(x,y,z,\phi)=(a\cos\omega t,a\sin\omega t,ut,\Omega t)$, the null condition and the three-dimensional curvature/torsion are
\begin{align}
a^2\omega^2+u^2+R^2\Omega^2&=1,\\
v_3^2&=a^2\omega^2+u^2,\\
\kappa&=\frac{a\omega^2}{v_3^2},\qquad \tau=\frac{u\omega}{v_3^2},\\
\boxed{v_3^2(\kappa^2+\tau^2)&=\omega^2}.
\end{align}
\section{Circular fiber and gauge invariance}
\begin{align}
 ds_5^2&=g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu+R^2(d\phi+kA_\mu dx^\mu)^2,\\
 \phi'&=\phi-k\chi,\qquad A'_\mu=A_\mu+\partial_\mu\chi,\\
 \mathcal R_5&=\mathcal R_4-\frac{R^2k^2}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\quad(R=\text{const}).
\end{align}
The restricted four-dimensional action takes the form
\begin{equation}
 S_{4,\mathrm{restricted}}=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{\mathcal R_4}{16\pi G_4}-\frac{R^2k^2}{64\pi G_4}F^2\right],\quad G_4=\frac{G_5}{2\pi R}.
\end{equation}
If $S_A=-\frac{\hbar}{4g^2}\int\sqrt{-g}F^2$, then $g^2=16\pi G_4\hbar/(R^2k^2)$.
\section{Radion obstruction}
For a variable radius $\rho$, the Jordan frame bulk Lagrangian is proportional to $\rho\mathcal R_4-(k^2\rho^3/4)F^2$, so
\begin{equation}
\mathcal R_4-\frac{3k^2\rho^2}{4}F^2=0.
\end{equation}
In source-free pure five-dimensional Einstein theory, freezing $\rho$ imposes the trace $\mathcal R_4=0$, leading to $F^2=0$; a generic electromagnetic configuration requires additional radion dynamics/sources.
\section{Mass and charge mismatch in the minimal model}
For a five-dimensional massless scalar $\Phi(x,\phi)=\sum_n\psi_n(x)e^{in\phi}$,
\begin{align}
D_\mu&=\partial_\mu-in kA_\mu,&m_n&=\frac{|n|\hbar}{R},\\
\alpha_n&=\frac{n^2k^2g^2}{4\pi}=\frac{4n^2G_4\hbar}{R^2}=\boxed{\frac{4G_4 m_n^2}{\hbar}}.
\end{align}
The observed electron mass, if incorrectly assigned to this simplest charged scalar KK mode, would imply $\alpha_{KK}\approx7.00724\times10^{-45}$, versus observed $\alpha\approx7.29735\times10^{-3}$. Thus the naive identification is not viable; extra matter dynamics are indispensable.
\section{Scientific status}
This mathematical construction yields only a geometrical route to gravity and $U(1)$, with a dynamical radion and a quantitative minimal-model obstruction. It does not establish weak/strong force unification, the Standard Model spectrum, or the microscopic source of $G$.
