\[ \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_c + \mathbf{v}_l = \omega 	imes \mathbf{r} + v_0 \mathbf{e}_z \]

曲率：\[ \kappa = \frac{\|\omega\|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|^2} \]
