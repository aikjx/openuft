# Limitations / scope fence
- Synthetic test vectors are mathematical demonstrations, not experimental data.
- Nulling assumes the chosen nuisance basis is correct and stable.
- Weighting can magnify noise and calibration errors; covariance must be measured.
- No quantum gravity detection, graviton evidence, or unification result is claimed.
- The PN/EFT numerical figures from V89 were not rederived here from the complete experimental geometry because that geometry and source protocol were not supplied as machine-readable inputs.
