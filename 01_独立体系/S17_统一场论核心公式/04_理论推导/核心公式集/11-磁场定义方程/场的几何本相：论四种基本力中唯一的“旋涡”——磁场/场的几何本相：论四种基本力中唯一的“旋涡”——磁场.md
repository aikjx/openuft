$$\mathbf{A} = -\frac{GM}{r^2} \hat{\mathbf{r}} \tag{8}$$

This is a radial, centripetally directed acceleration field with zero curl:

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{A} = 0 \quad \text{(for static spherically symmetric fields)} \tag{9}$$
