UFT reveals striking mathematical similarities between gravitational and electromagnetic field definitions:

- Electric field: \(\overrightarrow{E} = -\nabla\phi_e - \frac{\partial\overrightarrow{A_e}}{\partial t}\)

Both contain gradient of scalar potential and time derivative of vector potential, suggesting a common origin.

**Step 3: Rotational Coupling Mechanism**

We introduce a cross product relationship between gravitational and magnetic fields:

$$\overrightarrow{A} \times \overrightarrow{B} = \overrightarrow{C}$$ 

This cross product ensures directional interaction, consistent with vector field properties, representing transverse effects in field interactions.

**Step 4: Relativistic Covariance and Dimensional Analysis**

Ensuring relativistic covariance and dimensional consistency:
- Electric displacement relation: \(\overrightarrow{D} = \epsilon_0\overrightarrow{E}\)
- Speed of light relation: \(\mu_0 = \frac{1}{c^2\epsilon_0}\)

Substituting into Ampère-Maxwell law gives:

$$\nabla \times \overrightarrow{B} = \frac{1}{c^2\epsilon_0}\overrightarrow{j} + \frac{1}{c^2}\frac{\partial\overrightarrow{E}}{\partial t}$$ 

**Step 5: Construction of Unification Equation**

Through symmetry analysis and dimensional matching, we derive the unification equation:

$$\overrightarrow{A} \times \overrightarrow{B} = \frac{c^{2}}{\epsilon_0}\overrightarrow{j} + \frac{1}{\epsilon_0}\frac{\partial\overrightarrow{D}}{\partial t}$$ 

This equation unifies gravitational and electromagnetic fields, with the left side representing field-field interaction and the right side representing source terms, connected through spatial state changes.
