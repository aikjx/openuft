------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E391  TUFT isotropic metric
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ds^2 = -A(r) dt^2 + B(r)(dr^2 + r^2 dOmega^2),   G=c=M=1
  A(r) = exp(-2/r)
  B(r) = exp(+2/r) * (1 + c_m/r^2 + d/r^3)
  wall (B=0): r_h^3 + c_m r_h + d = 0

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E392  Chandrasekhar odd-parity general form
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ds^2 = -e^{2nu}dt^2 + e^{2lambda}dr^2 + r^2 dOmega^2
  d2psi/dr*^2 + [omega^2 - V_odd] psi = 0,  dr*/dr = exp(lambda-nu)=sqrt(B/A)
  V_odd = e^{2nu} * [ l(l+1)/r^2 - 6 M_eff/r^3 ]
  M_eff = (r/2)(1 - e^{-2lambda}) = (r/2)(1 - 1/B)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E393  TUFT explicit potential (l=2)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  V(r) = A/r^2 * [6 - 3(1 - 1/B)] = A/r^2 * (3 + 3/B)
  Old (erratum #21): V_old = A*6/r^2  (missing spin term)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E394  Scalar source: no odd-parity coupling
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Spin-0 scalar background h=1/r couples only to EVEN (polar) parity.
  Odd-parity needs odd-parity matter source (none for spin-0).
  => No extra scalar term in odd channel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E395  Wall boundary: B->0, V->inf, regularity=Neumann
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  At wall: 1/B->inf, V~1/(r*-r*_h)^2 (centrifugal barrier at regular centre).
  l=2 regularity: psi~(r*-r*_h)^3 => dpsi/dr*|wall=0 (Neumann W1).
  => |R|^2 = 1 for all frequencies (ideal wall, flux conservation).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E396  Tortoise coordinate
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  dr*/dr = sqrt(B/A) = exp(2/r) sqrt(1 + c_m/r^2 + d/r^3),  r*(r_h)=0
