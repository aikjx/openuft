# 可独立核验的公开参考资料

1. Dirac operators on the Taub-NUT space, monopoles and SU(2) representations (JHEP, 2014), in particular twisted Dirac operators on S2: https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4879
2. Lecture notes on index theorem on S2, including spinor bundle O(n-1): https://matfiz.github.io/topologija/en/lectures/28/
3. Effective field theory for magnetic compactifications: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03798
4. Toward Realistic Models in T2/Z2 Flux Compactification (2024): https://academic.oup.com/ptep/article/2024/5/053B06/7667937
5. Standard Model anomaly cancellation educational exercise by David Tong: https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/sm/rw3.pdf

这些来源用于核对已知数学/理论结构；ROOT 与具体自然界现象之间的连接仍需独立实验检验。
