# 外部资料与对照范围

本轮从下列公开资料核对**已建立的物理结果**；这些资料并不证明 ROOT 假设。代码由本研究包独立编写并执行。

2. Claudia de Rham, *Massive Gravity*, Living Reviews in Relativity (2014). 质量为零的自旋2线性微分同胚规范对称性、质量模与自由度。https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrr-2014-7
3. *The Fierz–Pauli theory on curved spacetime at one-loop and its counterterms* (2025). 线性质量为零自旋2规范作用量。https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14083-3
4. LIGO teaching material: two transverse quadrupolar polarizations. https://dcc-lho.ligo.org/public/0034/G020007/000/G020007-00.pdf
5. *Gravitational-Wave Tests of General Relativity with Ground-Based Detectors and Pulsar-Timing Arrays*, Living Reviews / PMC. 不同自旋对应不同引力波极化几何。https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5255575/
6. *Analogue Gravity*, Living Reviews (2026), Weinberg–Witten 条件性障碍的适用范围。https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41114-026-00064-9
