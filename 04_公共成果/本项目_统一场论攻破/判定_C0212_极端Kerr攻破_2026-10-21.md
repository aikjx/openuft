$$r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-a^2}=M(1\pm\lambda),\quad \lambda=\sqrt{1-(a/M)^2}$$
$$A_H=8\pi M r_+=8\pi M^2(1+\lambda),\quad S_{BH}=\frac{A_H}{4}=2\pi M^2(1+\lambda)\ \text{(Planck 单位)}$$
$$T_H=\frac{r_+-r_-}{4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}=\frac{\lambda}{4\pi M(1+\lambda)}$$
