# ROOT V72 参考来源与边界

以下是用来核对已确立物理与实验约束的资料。公开论文支持其中的标准概念，**并不支持 ROOT 新力已被发现的结论**。

1. NIST/CODATA 2022 Fundamental Constants: G = 6.67430(15)×10⁻¹¹ SI; c exact 299792458 m/s; https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/ ；论文汇总 https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/JPCRD2022CODATA.pdf 。
2. Touboul et al., "MICROSCOPE Mission: Final Results of the Test of the Equivalence Principle," Physical Review Letters 129, 121102 (2022), DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.129.121102, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36179190/ 。
3. Rutgers University Gauge Theory Lectures, "Field strength as commutator of covariant derivatives," https://www.physics.rutgers.edu/~shapiro/615/lects/LGFT1.pdf (背景教材；如需逐页审查PDF，应按PDF阅读流程)。
4. "Lu+ optical frequency references with accuracy verified at the 19th digit," Nature (2026), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11072-8 ，用于说明引力红移与毫米高度差是实在的高精度效应。
5. "Constraints on axion dark matter by distributed intercity quantum sensors," Nature (2026), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10034-w ，用于说明自旋/轴子样新物理搜索的实验技术。
6. PDG Review of Particle Physics, https://pdg.lbl.gov/ ，用于标准模型、耦合常数和新物理检验的背景基准。

常数与行星环境近似值来自早前版本的示例参数，且独立参见 NASA Solar System Planetary Fact Sheets。行星数据不用于推导 G，亦不作为精密地磁/真实密度模型。

引用符号 E=已有物理定律；C=在明确假设下的条件推导；H=模型假说；O=尚未解决。
