# 学术依据与验证边界

1. Particle Data Group (2026), Review of Particle Physics: https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/ ；具体标准模型/GUT 入口 https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/standard_model_and_related.html 及 https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/searches-and-hypothetical-particles.html 。用于说明四力统一的实测与理论状态，不代表 ROOT 已得到证实。
2. CERN, Standard Model overview: https://home.web.cern.ch/science/physics/standard-model 。用于区分已确立的电弱/强规范场论与未完成的引力统一。
3. Clifford/spin geometry lecture notes, MIT: https://math.mit.edu/~rbm/Loops2024/Isaac1.pdf ；Dirac operator and Lichnerowicz formula background.
4. Schrödinger–Lichnerowicz historical and mathematical review: https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10765
5. Curved Dirac and gauge field square: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06723

注意：本包没有使用真实卫星、新粒子或高能碰撞原始观测数据，未尝试从引用中提取未经核实的数值测量上界。
